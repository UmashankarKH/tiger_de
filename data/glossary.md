# CPG naming glossary

Abbreviations used in our warehouse column and table names.

- prd: product
- brnd: brand
- catg: category
- cust: customer
- cntct: contact
- phn: phone
- rgn: region
- chnl: channel
- sls: sales
- qty: quantity
- sld: sold
- rev: revenue
- amt: amount
- disc: discount
- prm: promotion
- flg: flag (Y/N)
- cd: code
- nm: name
- dt: date
- lnch: launch
- actv: active
- sz: size
- ml: millilitres
- uom: unit of measure
- std: standard
- lmt: limit
- tier: customer tier (A = key account, B = regional, C = long tail)
- id: identifier

## Business rules

- Net revenue = gross revenue - discount.
- Trade promotion flag = Y when the sale happened under a trade promotion.
- Channel codes: MT = Modern Trade, GT = General Trade, EC = E-commerce.
- Standard cost is the planned unit cost used for costing, not the actual cost.
